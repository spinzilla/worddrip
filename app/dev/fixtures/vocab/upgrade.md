## 词汇升级
| 日常/口语 | 学术升级 | 例句 | 出处 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| be made up of | **comprise**（不接 of）/ consist of / be composed of | comprise: The committee comprises five departments. (AI) ⧉ consist of: The committee consists of five departments. (AI) ⧉ be composed of: The committee is composed of five departments. (AI) | AWL SL7 | 9.18 期 |
| exact and clear | **precise**：precise definition, precise nature | precise: Precise measurements are essential in experimental work. | AWL SL5 + ACL | 9.18 期 |
| take account for（错） | **account for**（占比例，前面什么都不加） | account for: Women account for 40% of the workforce. (AI) | 作文实战 | 10.09 雅思T1 |
| 坏行 | 只有两列 |
