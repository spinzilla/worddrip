## 词汇升级
| 日常/口语 | 学术升级 | 例句 | 出处 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| be made up of | **comprise**（不接 of）/ consist of | comprise: The committee comprises five departments. (AI) ⧉ consist of: The committee consists of five departments. | AWL SL7 | 9.18 期；保留完整备注，不从详情截断文本拼回。第二段例句也要原样保留。 |
| exact and clear | **precise**：precise definition | precise: Precise measurements are essential in experimental work. (AI) | AWL SL5 + ACL | 多段例句：precise: First sentence. ⧉ precise: Second sentence. (AI) |
| be made up of | **consist of**（重复日常词） | consist of: The course consists of six modules. (AI) | 作文实战 | 重复词仍保留第二行 |
## 写作自查对照

- concise（简明）↔ verbose（啰嗦）：改稿时删 verbose expression
  - hedging（留有余地）↔ overgeneralization（过度概括）：检查 claim 是否过强
